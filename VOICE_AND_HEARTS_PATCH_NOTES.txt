Alpha Mate - Voice Playback & Real-time Hearts Patch

Base: AlphaMate_Auth_SFU_Fix.zip (latest uploaded project archive in this conversation)

Changes:
1. Added src/services/liveHeartService.ts for authenticated Firestore heart events and atomic persistent heart counters for liveStreams and partyRooms.
2. Updated LiveStreamScreen to publish a heart event on double tap, receive other users' heart events, show a live total counter, and route viewer audio through a dedicated audio-only element instead of relying on the video element's audio output.
3. Updated PartyRoomsScreen to publish/receive room heart events, show a live room heart count, and unlock remote audio playback on user interaction.
4. Updated PartyRemoteAudioPlayer to avoid forcibly enabling receiver tracks (the publisher's mute state must remain authoritative) and added preload behavior.
5. Updated Firestore rules to allow authenticated heart-event creation and controlled +1 counter updates for active live streams and party rooms.

Required deployment:
- Deploy the updated firestore.rules to Firebase.
- Build and deploy the updated frontend/backend repository.
- Test on two physical Android devices. A successful build is not proof of working two-way audio.
