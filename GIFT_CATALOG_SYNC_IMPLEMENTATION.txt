ALPHA MATE — GIFT CATALOG SYNC IMPLEMENTATION

Updated source: 24 Nov 2026

What changed
1. Normal Admin gift documents now normalize status case-insensitively and recognize Active/Published/Enabled/Show/Visible. Explicit false/0/no/hidden/off values in show, isActive, enabled, or visible keep the gift hidden.
2. Advanced gifts now normalize status case-insensitively. Draft, Processing, Disabled, Inactive, Hidden, Rejected, Archived, and Paused are not published. Active, Published, Enabled, Show, Visible, and Approved are published.
3. Advanced gift media URL resolution now accepts optimizedFileUrl, animationUrl, assetUrl, downloadUrl, and originalFileUrl consistently. A published advanced gift still needs a non-empty usable media URL to enter the send-gift gallery.
4. The unified gallery continues to merge the regular gifts collection and advancedGifts collection by document ID. An explicit inactive/hidden regular Admin catalog row suppresses a same-ID advanced gift so paused gifts cannot reappear.

Firestore collection contract
- Simple/standard gifts: gifts/{giftId}
  Recommended fields: name, coins (or costInCoins), icon, status, show, isActive, enabled, visible, priority/order, thumbnailUrl/imageUrl.
- Advanced animated gifts: advancedGifts/{giftId}
  Required to appear in App send-gift gallery: published/active status plus at least one valid media URL among optimizedFileUrl, animationUrl, assetUrl, downloadUrl, originalFileUrl.
  Optional presentation fields include format, displayMode, opacity, durationMs/duration, loop, playbackSpeed, position, scale, priority, enableSound, enableParticles, edgeFeather, blendMode.

Important
- Admins may list draft/processing/inactive records for management; that does not mean those records are eligible to appear in the App gallery.
- No Firestore records were changed by this source update. Push/deploy this app source, then verify the same gift IDs and media URLs in the live Firestore collections.
